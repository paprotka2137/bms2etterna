mongolskin (kb7)

Converted from the osu!mania skin "#마지막 스킨 [Stellium Skin]" by Applemango,
using the [Mania] Keys: 7 section of its Skin.ini.

- rounded notes: white, blue, white, gold, white, blue, white (mania-note1/2/S)
- grey LN bar with a rounded end (Notes4K/LN.png), LN head = the note
- outlined key frame on the hit position (mania/arrows/key1b, key1Db when pressed)
- notes are timed on their front edge like in osu!, not their centre
- no hit bursts, no stage hint, no column lights (all empty in the osu! skin)
- mines: osu! has none, so they are a darkened note with an X

Note size: 64 x 38 units (osu! proportions). Change NOTE_HEIGHT in Noteskin.lua
if you resize the note images.
